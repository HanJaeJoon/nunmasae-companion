## Full description (4,000)

A spoiler-free companion for readers of a long fantasy saga. Tell the app how far you have read, and it shows only the characters, races, places and terms that have appeared by then. Inside each entry you see only the facts revealed up to that book. Anything past your progress is not blurred or counted; it is simply not there.

Nunmasae Companion is an unofficial fan companion for the Korean fantasy novel "The Bird That Drinks Tears".

What's inside
- Characters, Races & Gods, Places and Terms tabs
- Name search across all tabs
- Each entry lists its facts in the order they come to light
- Pick your progress (Book 1 to 4) and change it any time
- Korean edition volumes shown next to the matching English edition volumes

Content
This version covers all four books of the Korean edition. Entries are still being checked, and reported mistakes are fixed quickly.

Languages
English and Korean, switchable in Settings.

Privacy and cost
- Works fully offline. No account, no server.
- No ads, no in-app purchases, no donations.
- Only your reading progress and language choice are stored, on your device.
